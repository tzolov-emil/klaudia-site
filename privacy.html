Klaudia — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 · Klaudia home

1. Who we are

Klaudia is a private work assistant operated by Emil Tzolov (Pixea AD, EIK 207403511, Sofia, Bulgaria) for his own use only. It is not offered to the public. Contact: tzolov.emil@gmail.com.

2. Google data we access and why

With Emil's consent, Klaudia accesses only Emil's own Google accounts (tzolov.emil@gmail.com and tzolov@pixea.eu):

  • Gmail — to read incoming messages and attachments, sort them by project, save attachments to Emil's project folders, create draft replies, add labels and move messages to trash when Emil asks. Klaudia never sends email.
  • Google Calendar — to show Emil's events and add events to his own calendar (without inviting other people).
  • Google Drive — to search, download and upload files in Emil's Drive. Klaudia does not share or delete Drive files.

3. Where the data is stored

Access tokens and all downloaded content are stored only on Emil's own computer. There is no server, database or cloud storage operated by Klaudia, and no other person has access.

4. Processing by an AI model

To read and summarise messages and documents, the relevant content is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI model under Emil's own Anthropic account, solely to perform the task Emil asked for. It is not used for advertising and is not sold.

5. Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except the processing described in section 4. It is not used for advertising, credit-worthiness or any purpose unrelated to Emil's own work.

6. Limited Use

Klaudia's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Retention and deletion

Data stays on Emil's computer until he deletes it. Access can be removed at any time in Klaudia (“Връзки” → “Прекъсни”) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions; this deletes the stored access tokens.

8. Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with a new date.
